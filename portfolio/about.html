THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Curious by nature.
Creative by practice.

I'm Eranga Perera, a Sri Lankan creative designer with experience across brand identity, graphic design, advertising and e-commerce marketing.

MY STORY

Design is how
I think out loud.

My work sits at the intersection of design and communication. I enjoy taking a brief, finding the idea at its centre and translating it into a visual language that feels distinctive, useful and memorable.

Since starting my creative career in 2016, I've worked across newspaper publishing, retail marketing, e-commerce and freelance projects. That mix has shaped the way I approach design: with attention to craft, an understanding of the audience and respect for the purpose behind every piece.

Today, I work as a Senior Creative Designer at Abans PLC, contributing to e-commerce and digital marketing creative across a broad range of consumer and lifestyle brands.
